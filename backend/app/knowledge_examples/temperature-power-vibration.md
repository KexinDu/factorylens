# Oil temperature abnormalities

FactoryLens educational demonstration content. Compare mean oil temperature with similar load and warm-up conditions. Higher temperature can accompany extended operation, increased losses or reduced cooling. Review cooler operation, ambient conditions and the temperature sensor record before considering a maintenance action.

## Motor power abnormalities

Mean motor power can increase with hydraulic demand or losses. Interpret electrical input power alongside pressure and volume flow, and check that comparable load and operating speed were used. Review indications of supply or motor issues with qualified personnel.

## Vibration abnormalities

Higher vibration velocity can accompany changes in load, mounting, rotating components or fluid conditions. Verify sensor mounting and compare readings at a similar operating state. A frequency spectrum and time trace can help qualified personnel investigate, if available. A cycle average alone cannot identify cavitation, imbalance or bearing damage.
